Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, verified answers to common questions about Zhatura AI Chess Coach

Zhatura is an AI-powered chess learning platform designed for students, children, parents, chess coaches, and academies. Unlike simple chess-playing apps, Zhatura pairs interactive learning with the Zhatura AI Chess Coach and specialized dashboards for students, parents, and coaches.

The AI Chess Coach supports a child's learning journey by explaining chess principles in simple terms, pointing out why a move was a mistake, and recommending better strategic ideas during review.

Students log in through the Student Dashboard. If you experience sign-in issues with a specific child account, our Customer Support team at 095-138-86363 can note your ticket and assist.

For current plan or pricing information, please contact Zhatura Customer Support directly at 095-138-86363. Our customer service team will share current details, discounts, and organization options.

Our customer care line can converse fluently in English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, and Odia.

Never. Zhatura AI customer care and staff never request passwords, OTPs, or payment card CVVs. Please never share security credentials with anyone.
