# Technical Support

## What the AI support agent can help with
The AI support agent can answer general verified questions about Zhatura, its dashboards and the AI Chess Coach, and it can guide callers to the right support path. It cannot access accounts or perform technical fixes.

## App or dashboard not loading
If the app or dashboard is not loading, general checks such as internet connection, browser refresh or restarting the app may help. Specific platform recovery steps are not yet documented in verified sources.

## Lesson or session not visible
If a lesson or session is not visible, this may be account-specific. The assistant cannot see the user's account or session status. Explain how sessions normally work in general, then offer a support next step.

## Audio or voice issue
Audio and voice issues during a phone call may relate to the caller's device, network or the telephony connection. The assistant can note the issue for the support team.

## When to escalate
Escalate to human support when the caller needs account access, billing help, technical fixes, or any action the AI assistant cannot perform in this phase.
