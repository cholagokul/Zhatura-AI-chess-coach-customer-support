# Parent Features

## Parent Dashboard and progress
Zhatura has a Parent Dashboard. Parents can view their child's chess learning progress in the Parent Dashboard.

## How Zhatura helps parents
Zhatura can help parents support a child's chess learning, review activity where supported, manage consent and protected features, and provide a safer learning environment for younger players.

## Guardian controls
Zhatura includes guardian-oriented controls for younger players. Documented capabilities include managing consent, reviewing activity, and controlling access to protected features. These controls are designed to help parents and guardians stay involved in their child's learning.
