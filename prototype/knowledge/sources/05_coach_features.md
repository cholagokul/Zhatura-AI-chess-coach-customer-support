Zhatura has a Coach Dashboard. Coaches manage batches in the Coach Dashboard.

Zhatura is designed to support chess coaching workflows and player development. Documented capabilities include managing batches through the Coach Dashboard, alongside platform features such as online play, game review and guided learning that coaches can use with their students.
