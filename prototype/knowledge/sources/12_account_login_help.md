# Account and Login Help

## Login flow
Students, parents and coaches log in through their respective dashboards. Students log in through the Student Dashboard.

## Forgotten password
Specific password-reset steps, email or mobile recovery flows, and account-recovery contacts are not yet documented in verified Zhatura sources. Do not guess a reset URL or process.

## Role-based login
Zhatura serves students, parents, coaches, academies and organizations. Each role has a dashboard entry point, but the exact login roles and role-switching behaviour are not yet documented in verified sources.

## Account creation
How accounts are created — for example by a parent, school, coach or self-signup — is not yet documented in verified sources.

## Common login errors
If a caller reports a login error, the assistant cannot view or fix their account in this phase. Offer general guidance such as checking credentials and internet, then escalate to the support team.
