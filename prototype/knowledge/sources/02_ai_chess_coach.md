# Zhatura AI Chess Coach

## What the AI Chess Coach is
The Zhatura AI Chess Coach is Zhatura's AI coach for learning chess. Children use the AI Chess Coach as part of the Zhatura learning platform. It exists alongside the Student, Parent and Coach Dashboards.

## My Zhatura Coach
The AI coaching experience is called My Zhatura Coach. My Zhatura Coach helps players reflect on completed games. It provides supportive explanations and reflective questions that help players understand their decisions — why a move was played, what alternatives might have existed, what the player noticed, what the player may have missed, and how to improve similar decisions in future games. It is a learning aid and does not replace a human chess coach.

## What the AI Chess Coach can explain generally
The AI Chess Coach supports a child's chess learning journey. It can explain chess concepts, point out mistakes and suggest what to focus on next during practice and review.

## Post-game learning focus
Zhatura's AI coaching is designed for learning and post-game reflection, not for giving players moves during an active competitive game. My Zhatura Coach does not provide a move, the best move, or any move suggestions while you are playing a competitive game. Its coaching focuses on a small number of meaningful moments after a game instead of overwhelming learners with every possible chess mistake, so the learner can focus on the most useful moments from a game.
