# Escalation and Human Support

## What the AI can help with
The AI support agent can answer general verified questions about Zhatura, its dashboards and the AI Chess Coach. It can also guide callers to the right support path.

## What requires human support
Human support is needed for account access, billing questions, payment issues, refund requests, technical fixes the AI cannot perform, and complex complaints.

## What requires account access
Any question about the caller's own account, child profile, subscription, payment or today's lesson/session status requires account access. The AI does not have account access in this phase.

## What requires billing support
Pricing, plan changes, payment failures, refund requests, invoice questions and subscription problems require billing support.

## What requires technical support
App or dashboard not loading, persistent audio issues, lesson or session visibility problems, and login errors may require technical support.

## What requires organization sales or custom plans
Academy bulk signups, organization custom plans and enterprise pricing require the sales or organization support team. Details are not yet documented in verified sources.

## How escalation works today
Live transfer to a human agent is not enabled on this phone line yet. The support flow notes the issue for the support team, who can follow up with the caller.
