# Privacy and Safety

## Safety features for young players
Zhatura includes safety and privacy measures for young players. Documented safety-oriented features include private profiles, restricted communication, reporting, and blocking. These features are designed to help provide a more suitable learning environment for younger players.

## What the assistant will not ask for
The assistant never asks for passwords, OTPs, sensitive payment-card information, or any other confidential account details on a call.

## Account access in this phase
The assistant cannot log into, view, check or modify any individual customer account, child profile, subscription, payment or lesson/session status in the current phase.

## Guardian consent and protected features
Parents and guardians can manage consent and control access to protected features as part of the documented guardian controls. Reporting and blocking are available as safety tools for players.

## Data handling claims
Specific data-retention periods, encryption standards, compliance certifications or third-party subprocessors are not yet documented in verified Zhatura sources. Do not claim certification, legal compliance or security standards that are not verified.

## Reporting privacy concerns
Privacy concerns can be noted for the support team, who can follow up through the appropriate channel once human support is enabled.
